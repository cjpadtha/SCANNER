How to read this

With Kalshi connected, the headline is Kalshi's own probability: in our tests it called the window right more often than any model we built (70% vs 67% at minute 5, 83% vs 80% at minute 11). Without it, the headline is our estimate: the chance BTC ends above the strike, from its distance to the strike, the last hour's volatility and the time left. In our 4-year test Kalshi's own price picked winners slightly better (65% vs 62%) and trading the gaps lost about 1¢ per contract after fees. Use it to read the lean at a glance and to avoid overpaying. "Expensive" means skip. Minute-by-minute momentum and order flow tested at 47–49%: no edge, so they're shown as context only. Kalshi settles on the CF Benchmarks index; Coinbase is a close proxy.
