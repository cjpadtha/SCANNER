This estimate uses BTC's distance from the strike, its volatility over the last hour and the time left. In our 4-year test, Kalshi's own price was the better predictor (65% vs 62% winners) and trading the gaps lost ~1¢ per contract after fees. Use it to see the lean and avoid overpaying, not as a guaranteed edge. Kalshi settles on the CF Benchmarks BTC index; Coinbase is a close proxy, so tiny gaps near the strike can go either way.
